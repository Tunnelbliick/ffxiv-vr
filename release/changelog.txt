# Pass the prerelease flag to gh as a value
Splatting an array that held --prerelease made gh read a stray "-" as
an asset path, which failed the fork release after the tag was pushed.
--prerelease=true|false works the same for forks and upstream.

# Map third-person tracking to the body, add seated play and start SteamVR
In third person, motion tracking and the shared pose now place your
hands and head relative to your body's heading instead of the camera, so
they no longer flip when you look at your character from the front.
Third-person motion tracking is a setting, on by default, and the
shared pose is sent in third person either way. The body heading
follows the head past 45 degrees; with the new Seated play option it
stays at the recenter forward and sitting low no longer reads as a bow.

Starting VR while SteamVR is closed now launches SteamVR and turns VR
on once it is running, instead of letting OpenXR start it mid-session.

Camera shake drops non-finite frames and resets, so a degenerate game
view indoors can no longer latch NaN and black out the world.

# Let the release workflow publish from forks
Download links and RepoUrl now come from the repository the workflow
runs in, and the changelog starts at the latest reachable v* tag. On a
fork, a release bumps the fourth version number, keeps the fork's own
listing name, description and punchline, and is marked as a prerelease.
Upstream releases behave as before.
