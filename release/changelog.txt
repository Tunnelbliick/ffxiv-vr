# Pass vertical shake to third person and allow up to 10x shake
The level and floor-locked third-person cameras dropped the vertical part
of the game's screen shake, so stomps didn't shake the VR view; they now
add the height of the look-at offset. The Camera shake setting goes up to
10x, with the scaled shake capped, and the debug window can play a test
shake.
