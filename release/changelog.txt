# Keep the torso upright when looking up
Looking up at a taller character could hunch the shared body, since the
spine leaned toward the headset without regard to where the head looked.
After the lean, the spine now straightens until the chest faces no more
than 45 degrees below the head's aim (or 50 degrees above it when looking
down), like VRIK's chest clamp, and the neck turns relative to the chest
so it always sits between the chest and the head.

# Carry the game's camera quake into VR
Stomps and other battle-effect camera quakes shake the game's view matrix,
which VR replaced, so the shake never reached the headset. The plugin now
reads the quake's offset, pitch, yaw and roll from that matrix and adds
them back in every camera mode, scaled by the Camera shake setting. It
also no longer reads back its own view, which made the screen tilt at
high shake strengths. The debug window can test each source of shake
separately. Quakes from other players only play when the game's Battle
Effects setting for others allows it.
